Advanced Program in Information System

An introduction of the Advanced Program in Information Systems

The approval of the project “Renovation in Vietnamese higher education” by the Prime Minister (according to the Resolution No.  14/2005/NQ-CP on November 2, 2005) is considered to be one of the most outstanding educational events in 2005. With a persistent 15-year strategy (from 2006 to 2020), the government aims at advancing the entire system so it can be compared with that of other areas and approach to international education. At a result, the Education and Training department assigned the University of Information Technology to conduct the Advanced Program with the Information System major in 2007 (according to Resolution No. 7581/QD-BGDDT), including following goals

  • To establish higher education curriculum which can approach to standard quality of advanced universities in the world
  • To promote interaction and integration in training as well as scientific research; synchronously improve the training conduction and expertise of management employees and teaching employees
  • To facilitate students in the progress of systematically acquiring and improving knowledge; developing reasoning ability, enhancing vocational skills and foreign language proficiency so that students can address society needs after graduation
  • To construct and upgrade facilities at the aim of serving training and increasing science technology research along with international collaboration

The success of this model can lead to a breakthrough in completely renovating training program of the University and conducting effective international collaboration

Plan for Implementation

  • Conduct full time training at Information System faculty belonged to University of Information Technology – Vietnam National University – Ho Chi Minh City
  • Correspond the training courses to those at Oklahoma State University – the United State with two specific information systems as Management Information System (MIS) and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
  • Utilize English in teaching entire subjects

Classroom and laboratory system

  • Construct two classrooms exclusively for Advanced Program students with modern facilities such as projectors and air-conditioners
  • Construct modern laboratories with high configuration computers and internet speed exclusively for Advanced Program students’ learning process

Teaching Staff

  • Professional lecturers from University of Information Technology, member Universities of Ho Chi Minh city National University, Oklahoma State University, and other foreign Universities.
  • English proficient lecturers with Master and Doctorate degrees issued by schools of English speaking countries or experienced teachers from other correspondent programs

 Transfer Opportunity to OSU

After the completion of two academic years at the University of Information Technology, students can transfer to OSU in the third year.

OSU accepts all of the credits which are finished at the University of Information Technology

Facilities and Learning Materials

Library

  • Foreign language textbooks
  • Foreign language reference books
  • Electronic library system of Ho Chi Minh city National University and foreign electronic library systems
  • Construct two classrooms exclusively for Advanced Program students with modern facilities such as projectors and air-conditioners
  • Construct modern laboratories with high configuration computers and internet speed exclusively for Advanced Program students’ learning process
